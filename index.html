How the typing test works

Focus the paragraph and start typing. The timer starts on your first keystroke. Finish the paragraph to complete the test — your WPM and accuracy update live.

  • Start: press Start or click the paragraph.
  • Regenerate: create a new paragraph anytime.
  • Restart: resets the current paragraph and timer.
